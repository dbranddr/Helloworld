and I'm a maritime solutions designer.

Simple and Clean Work Process

Maritime design is an exciting journey — and the process, consisting of six key steps, has been created to provide you with full support.

(01)

Initial Contact and Needs Assessment

The client gets in touch (by phone, form, or visit), and I gather basic information about the project and prepare an offer.

(02)

Detailed Consultation and Site Survey

After the offer and first payment, I visit the site and consult with the client to understand their preferences and maritime project expectations.

(03)

Concept Creation and Approval

I create up to three concept plans, refine the chosen one with the client, and proceed with the second payment upon approval.

(04)

Equipment and Material Selection

Detailed list of proposed equipment, vessels, and materials, present it to the client, and incorporate their feedback.

(05)

Visualizations and Final Adjustments

Project visualizations to help the client envision the maritime solution, then make final adjustments after review.

(06)

Documentation Handover

You finalize the complete conceptual maritime documentation. After handing it over, the final payment is made.
